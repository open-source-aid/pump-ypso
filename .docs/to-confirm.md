## Therapy and clock command choices (driver)

What the therapy and clock paths decide that no single source fully settles.

| # | Point | Driver today | How to settle |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | TBR blackout gating | RESOLVED. The basal-blackout wait before a TBR start is gated on `ver_control < 1.3`; the TBR-end (expiry) wait runs unconditionally, and the bolus-window wait stays unconditional. On a `control >= 1.3` pump the expiry wait alone already keeps a 15-min-aligned TBR off the 3-min boundary, so gating the basal wait leaves the net start delay unchanged | — |
| T2 | Basal ceiling with no reported limit | RESOLVED. Settings 113 (cap), 114 (min) and 115 (max) are read; only a value `< 0` means "no limit", and `0` is a literal zero limit (a `0` cap or max gives a zero ceiling; a `0` min is a zero lower bound). The effective ceiling is the smaller present one of 113/115; with neither present it is `i32::MAX / 10` hundredths (effectively unlimited) and the pump rejects at the write | — |
| T3 | Clock sync re-read | `SyncClock` writes with a 5 s midnight margin (time before date, `commands.rs`) and returns `Report::Done` without re-reading. Rockit re-reads to report drift-after; the sans-IO engine carries no drift in the outcome, so the re-read is omitted | confirm no caller needs a post-write clock read, else add it |
| T4 | Reconnect TBR status | after a reconnect `status()` reconstructs a running temp basal as `Basal::Temporary` from the pump's percent and the minutes-remaining field (`GetSystemStatus` offset 14), mirroring how a running extended bolus is reconstructed, instead of `Basal::Unknown` | confirm the minutes-remaining field is authoritative on hardware (bench item 4) |
| T5 | Pump mode access level | no firmware note covers the access level for a pump-mode (Suspend/Resume) write, so those commands stay `Unsupported` | probe a pump-mode write at each access level on hardware |
| T6 | Absolute temp basal strategy | an absolute rate is delivered as a native percent TBR when `round(100*rate/scheduled)` clamped to the percent cap lands within 5% of the request (`Completed` when exact, else `Approximated`); otherwise, and when the scheduled rate is zero, as a 0% TBR for the duration plus a 15-minute extension together with one native extended bolus of `rate*duration/60`, reported `Approximated`. Without the native extended bolus (`ver_control <= 1.1`) only the percent path runs. A rate the pump cannot represent within 5% either way is `Refused(OutOfLimits)`; the achievable rate is never silently under- or over-delivered | confirm the 5% acceptance band and the `Completed`/`Approximated` split match clinical expectations on the bench |
| T7 | Extended-bolus duration stretch/shrink | the emulated extended bolus starts at the temp-basal duration and is adjusted in 15-minute steps: stretched up to 60 minutes while the amount is below the minimum bolus, shrunk (bounded to 64 steps) while above the maximum bolus; the amount is then rounded to the bolus grid and clamped to [min bolus, max bolus]. The achieved rate reported is `amount*60/window` | confirm the exact 15-minute stepping, the 60-minute stretch ceiling and the grid rounding of the amount against pump delivery on the bench |
| T8 | Rate-controlled bolus sub-bolus floor | `RateBolus { amount, duration }` is delivered as a sequence of immediate boluses of the pump bolus step, spaced `duration / n` apart with the spacing clamped to `dt_min = 18 s` (the sub-bolus delay plus the basal blackout window plus the BLE round trip). `n = round(amount / step)`; a request whose spacing would fall below `dt_min` is coarsened to fewer, larger micro-boluses (`ceil(n / floor(duration / dt_min))` steps each) and refused `OutOfLimits` only when it cannot be spread over at least two micro-boluses or needs more than 256 of them. Each tick's due time is recomputed from the sequence start so delay does not accumulate | time the shortest safe gap between two immediate boluses on hardware (sub-bolus delay + blackout + round trip) and set `dt_min` to the measured floor |
| T9 | Rate-controlled bolus clock marker | after the last micro-bolus the clock is written back by `c = (delivered hundredths) mod 59` seconds and then forward to the true time, two `SystemTime` writes that stay within the same minute (never below second `c`) and so never cross midnight. The pair records two TIME_SET entries whose written-second difference is `c`; recovery sums the immediate boluses since the sequence start and checks the sum's `mod 59` against `c`. An absent marker means the sequence was interrupted, and the exact delivered amount is read back from history rather than re-delivered | confirm a backward-then-forward `SystemTime` write of a few seconds within one minute logs exactly two TIME_SET entries with no alarm, no daily total and no other side effect |
